Alliance EditionHow We Play

Phase 1 — The Journey

Casual · Social

No pre-reqs, no gear checks. Leveling, exploring, world content — the stuff that's just more fun with company. Show up when you can, no questions asked.

Difficulty: WelcomingAttendance: Whenever you're freeLoot: The journey itself

Phase 2 — Raid Night

Semi-core · Scheduled

A standing group that shows up on purpose, learns the pulls, and clears content together — without demanding your whole week to do it.

Difficulty: ScheduledAttendance: Most weeksLoot: Master-looted, split fair

Phase 3 — Progression

Core · Committed

The sweaty part, by choice. For the group chasing server-relevant kills and tighter execution — same guild, same Discord, different gear.

Difficulty: ProgressionAttendance: CommittedLoot: Prioritized for progression
