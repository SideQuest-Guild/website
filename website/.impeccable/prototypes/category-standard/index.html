About the Guild

Three pillars, one roster. Whether you're here for the company or the kill, SideQuest holds the door open.

Social

No attendance quota to belong here. Drop into Lobby, run old content for the story, or just talk shop between dungeons — the guild is a place first and a roster second.

Raiding

A semi-core raid team that shows up on a schedule and clears with intent. Structured enough to plan around, relaxed enough that one missed week won't cost you your spot.

Progression

For the roster chasing the next kill, progression raiding is here too — same guild, same Discord, a harder lockout. Pick the lane that fits your week; both count.
