Scope: the public homepage of the SideQuest guild site (one long page; the nav anchors to its sections). Visitor mode: Persuade. ## Direction contract

THESIS: One painted night carries the guild's whole premise: the side quest (campfire, casual) on the left, the raid (torchlit road to the citadel) on the right, and SideQuest between them. Refuses the flat-UI guild template and the game-UI costume; the art earns attention, the type stays thin and calm.

OWN-WORLD: Alliance night blue over charcoal stone; warm gold as the single accent (hairline rules with a pale four-point star, outlined diamonds, a gold plaque button). Original painted concept art with original heraldry (gold lion rampant on royal blue; never the official faction lion), white heraldic lion and gryphon around a gold star. Type: Libertinus Serif Display for wide-tracked caps (title, section heads), EB Garamond for everything else.

STORY: The visitor sees the campfire and the citadel and reads the two halves (casual, social, whenever you're on / scheduled nights, real progression) and the line that joins them: the journey and the raid both count. The fact strip gives launch, ruleset, region. Below: how we play (three paintings), raids (schedule, sign-ups, raid charts to come), addons (to come), the road opens (launch date, Discord channels), footer with the non-affiliation line.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed panorama to 87% of the frame. Top: hanging banner crest and SIDEQUEST wordmark left, four nav links right (How we play, Raids, Addons, Discord). Centre: lion and gryphon emblem, SIDEQUEST in wide caps, a star rule, the tagline, and the gold Join the Discord plaque. The side quest: at left over the forest, The raid: at right over the citadel. Bottom: a charcoal strip with a star rule and one line of facts flanked by short ornaments.

FORM: Painted-world gaming site (the user's Eldritch reference), comp-led from comp C. Signature interaction: the hero settles in (a slow push on the painting while the emblem, title, and the two halves resolve out of the mist), and each painting below lifts on hover.

RISK: Generic-template drift if the type is set heavy or the gold spreads; keep caps thin and tracked, gold confined to hairlines, stars and the one plaque.
